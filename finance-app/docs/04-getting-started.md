# 4. Getting started

This chapter takes you from an empty account to working books. Most steps are done once, usually by
the Chief Accountant and the System Administrator. Our team can do the set-up with you.

## Before you start

- Your organization has a Tatook workspace that includes Finance.
- Your **main currency** and **time zone** are set on the workspace.
- The people who will use Finance have been invited.

## Step 1 — Give people their roles

Give each person a Finance role (see [Roles, approvals and controls](03-roles-and-controls.md)).
A typical small team:

- Director → **Finance Manager**
- Accountant → **Chief Accountant**
- Officer who asks for payments → **Finance Officer**
- Person who pays and receives money → **Cashier**
- IT or operations → **System Administrator**

## Step 2 — Check your list of accounts

Open **Accounts**. A starting list is already there.

- Rename accounts to the names you use, and add any that are missing.
- Add **one account for each** bank account, cash box, mobile money wallet and card account you
  have.

## Step 3 — Add your projects and departments

Under **Dimensions**, add your departments or branches, and your projects (for example each donor
project).

## Step 4 — Set up your financial year

Under **Periods**, create your financial year (for example 1 January to 31 December).

## Step 5 — Enter your starting balances

Under **Opening balances**, enter what each account held on your first day: money in each bank and
cash box, what customers owed you, what you owed suppliers, your equipment, loans and so on. Do not
worry if it does not balance at first — you can correct the figures later.

If customers or suppliers had unpaid invoices or bills, enter those too, so they can be paid and
followed up.

## Step 6 — Add your tax rates

Under **Tax codes**, add the tax rates you charge on sales and pay on purchases.

## Step 7 — Set your approval rules

The System Administrator opens **Approval rules** and sets the payment approval limit, whether
payments are checked before approval, whether journals need approval, and the petty cash limit.

## Step 8 — Optional extras

- **Petty cash:** create a fund, choose its custodian, and request the payment that fills it.
- **Budgets:** create this year's budget, or copy last year's figures.
- **Repeating journals:** rent and other monthly entries.
- **Fixed assets** (if on your plan): add your vehicles, computers and equipment.
- **Currencies** (if on your plan): enter today's exchange rates.
- **Bank rules:** for bank charges that appear every month.

## Step 9 — Connect your other Tatook applications

If you use Pharmacy, Retail, Restaurant, Petrol, Payroll, Projects or Procurement, their sales,
purchases and payments start arriving in Finance automatically. See
[Connected applications](06-connected-applications.md).

## Your monthly checklist

1. Upload and check each bank statement.
2. Check and count petty cash, and top up the funds.
3. Clear payments and journals waiting for approval.
4. Record depreciation (if you have fixed assets) and update currency values (if you use other
   currencies).
5. Look at the profit and loss and the budget against actual.
6. File your tax return, if one is due.
7. Lock the month.

At the end of the year, once the last month is locked and the figures are agreed, **close the year**.
