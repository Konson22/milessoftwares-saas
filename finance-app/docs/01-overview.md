# 1. Overview

## What Tatook Finance is

Tatook Finance is accounting software that you use in your web browser. There is nothing to install.
You sign in with your Tatook account, the same one you use for your other Tatook applications.

Everything that involves money — a sale, a purchase, a payment, a transfer between bank accounts —
is recorded in one place, called the **ledger**. Every report you see is read from that ledger, so
all your figures agree with each other.

## Who it is for

- **Shops, pharmacies, restaurants and petrol stations** using Tatook, who want their sales,
  stock and cash to reach the accounts automatically.
- **NGOs and project-based organizations** who need every payment approved, and spending reported
  by project and by donor.
- **Finance teams of any size.** One accountant can run it alone. A bigger team can share the work
  between a Finance Manager, accountants, finance officers, cashiers and an auditor.

## Why you can trust the figures

**One set of books.** All your money records are in one place. There is no separate list
somewhere else that can disagree.

**Mistakes are corrected, never hidden.** Once an entry is saved, nobody can edit or delete it, not
even the owner. To fix a mistake, you add a correcting entry. Your history stays complete, which is
what auditors and donors want to see.

**Money leaves only with approval.** Every way money can leave your bank or cash goes through
approval once it is above the limit you set. Nobody can approve their own payment.

**Controls are built in.** Finished months can be locked so nobody changes them. Every important
action — who approved, who paid, who changed a setting — is recorded with the person's name and the
time.

**Amounts are exact.** Calculations, including currency conversions, are exact to the cent.

## What you can do with it

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Accounts | The list of categories your money is recorded under (for example "Rent", "Sales", "Bank") |
| Journals | Recording adjustments and corrections; repeating monthly entries such as rent |
| Customers | Sending invoices and recording what customers pay |
| Suppliers | Recording supplier bills and paying them |
| Payments | Asking for, approving and making payments |
| Banking | Bank, cash, mobile money and card accounts, and moving money between them |
| Bank checks | Comparing your bank statement with your records, line by line |
| Petty cash | Small cash spending, kept by one person and checked by another |
| Tax | Tax rates, tax returns and paying the tax office |
| Budgets | Planning income and spending month by month, then comparing with what happened |
| Fixed assets | Equipment and vehicles, and how their value goes down over time |
| Currencies | Working in more than one currency |
| Projects and departments | Seeing income and spending for each project, donor or department |
| Periods | Closing each month and each year |
| Reports | Profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow and more, downloadable to Excel |
| Dashboards | A quick view of the figures that matter to each person |
| Audit trail | A record of who did what, and when |

Fixed assets and multiple currencies are available on plans that include them.
