# 7. Questions and answers

**Do we need to install anything?**
No. Tatook Finance works in your web browser, so there is nothing to install. You sign in with your
Tatook account.

**Do we need to be accountants to use it?**
No. Most entries are created for you when you send an invoice, pay a bill or make a sale in a Tatook
shop application. An accountant is helpful for setting up and for month-end, and our team can help.

**Can I edit or delete an entry after saving it?**
No, and that protects you. To fix a mistake, click **Reverse** and enter the correct figures. Both
stay visible, so your history is complete.

**Can the owner approve their own payment?**
No. Nobody can approve or make a payment they asked for themselves. The owner follows the same rules
as everyone else.

**We are a small team. Can one person do everything?**
Yes. One person can have several roles. Tatook will warn you where this weakens your controls. You
can also set an approval limit so that small payments do not need approval.

**Which banks work with the bank statement check?**
Any bank that lets you download your statement as a CSV file, which most online banking does. Mobile
money statements work the same way.

**Do we have to enter our shop sales again in Finance?**
No. Sales, purchases, stock changes and cash movements from Tatook Pharmacy, Retail, Restaurant and
Petrol are recorded in Finance automatically.

**What happens to a sale on a day we have already locked?**
It is recorded on the first open day, so it is never lost. People cannot enter anything into a
locked month by hand.

**Can we work in more than one currency?**
Yes, on plans that include multiple currencies. Your reports are always in your main currency, and
gains or losses from exchange rates are worked out for you.

**Can we report per project or donor?**
Yes. Label amounts with a project or department, then see profit and loss, spending and budgets for
each one.

**Can we download reports?**
Yes. Every report, tax return and budget can be downloaded as an Excel file.

**How do we move from our old system?**
Set up your financial year, enter your starting balances, and add any unpaid invoices and bills. See
[Getting started](04-getting-started.md). Our team can do this with you.

**Can we reopen a year we have closed?**
Yes. The months stay locked until you unlock them one by one.

**Who can see salaries?**
Only the Payroll application. Finance receives totals only, never one person's pay.

**Is our data kept separate from other organizations?**
Yes. Your data belongs to your organization only, and every request is checked against the person's
membership and role.

**Is there a record of who did what?**
Yes. The audit trail records every approval, payment, correction, locked month, settings change and
petty cash action, with the person's name and the time.
