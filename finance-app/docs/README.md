# Tatook Finance

**Simple, safe accounting for your organization — from a single shop to an NGO with many projects.**

Tatook Finance is where your organization keeps its accounts. It records the money that comes in,
the money that goes out, what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers. From that, it gives
you clear reports: how much you made or spent, how much cash you have, and where it went.

It is part of Tatook, a product of Miles Global Link. If you already use another Tatook application
(Pharmacy, Retail, Restaurant, Petrol, Payroll, Projects or Procurement), its sales, purchases and
payments reach your accounts on their own. Nobody types them twice.

## Five things to know

- **Your figures always add up.** Every amount is recorded on both sides (where it came from and
  where it went), so the accounts always balance.
- **Nothing is secretly changed.** A saved entry cannot be edited or deleted. A mistake is fixed
  with a new correcting entry, so you can always see what happened.
- **No money leaves without approval.** Payments are prepared, approved and paid by different
  people, above a limit you choose.
- **It works the way you are paid.** Bank, cash, mobile money, card and petty cash.
- **Reports are ready when you need them.** Profit and loss, balance sheet, cash flow, budgets and
  more, each one downloadable to Excel.
