# 6. Connected applications

If you use other Tatook applications, they send what happens in them to Tatook Finance
automatically. A sale in your shop, a delivery from a supplier or a payroll run appears in your
accounts without anyone typing it again.

## Why you can rely on it

- **Recorded once.** Each sale or payment is recorded exactly once, even if the connection drops and
  it is sent again.
- **Nothing is lost.** If something arrives out of order, it waits and is recorded as soon as it can
  be.
- **Never blocked.** If a sale falls in a month you have already locked, it is recorded on the first
  open day instead of being refused.
- **Easy to trace.** Every entry shows which application and which document it came from.

## What each application sends

### Shops: Pharmacy, Retail, Restaurant and Petrol

| When this happens in your shop | Finance records |
| --- | --- |
| A sale | The sale, the tax, and what the customer owes |
| The customer pays (cash, mobile money, card, bank) | The money received in the right account |
| A sale is cancelled | The sale is undone, and any refund is recorded |
| A customer returns goods | The sale is reduced, and the refund is recorded |
| A supplier delivers goods | The stock received and what you owe the supplier |
| A supplier gives a credit note | What you owe the supplier is reduced |
| Your opening stock | The value of the stock you started with |
| A stock count finds a difference, or goods are written off | The loss (or gain) in stock value |
| The till count is short or over | The difference, as cash short or over |
| Cash is banked, taken from the bank, or used for an expense | The movement between cash and bank, or the expense |

### Payroll

For each approved payroll, Finance receives **one total**: salaries, taxes and deductions — and
later the payment of salaries from the bank. **Nobody can see individual salaries in Finance.**

### Projects

For NGOs and project-based organizations:

- When a project asks for money, the request appears in Finance under **Project requests**.
  Approval in Projects does not pay anything on its own: Finance's people still take it through the
  normal payment approval — or refuse it.
- The project team is kept informed at every step: received, approved, rejected, paid.
- Grants and project spending are reported per project.

### Procurement

Supplier invoices from Tatook Procurement arrive in Finance as bills. They are paid through payment
requests, with the same approvals as any other payment.

## Your accounts stay in charge

Whatever the source, the entries are part of your accounts like any other. They can be corrected,
they appear in every report, and they follow the same rules for locked months, tax and the audit
trail.
