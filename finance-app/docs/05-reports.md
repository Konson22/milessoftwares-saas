# 5. Reports

All reports come from the same records, so they always agree with each other. You can run them for
any date or period, and most can be shown for **one project or department only**. Every report can be
**downloaded to Excel**.

## The main financial reports

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Profit and loss** | How much you earned and spent in a period, and whether you made a profit or a loss. You can compare with an earlier period. |
| **Profit and loss by project or department** | The same, with one column for each project or department |
| **Balance sheet** | On a given day: what you own, what you owe, and what is left for the organization |
| **Cash flow** | Where your cash came from and where it went during a period |
| **Trial balance** | Every account and its balance on a given day — the accountant's check that everything adds up |

## Day-to-day reports

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Who owes you** (aged receivables) | Each customer's unpaid amount, and how late it is: not yet due, up to 30, 60, 90 days, or more |
| **Who you owe** (aged payables) | What you owe each supplier, in the same way |
| **Account history** (general ledger) | Every amount recorded on each account, with the running balance |
| **Tax return** | Tax collected, tax paid and the amount due to the tax office, by tax rate |

## Planning and performance

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Budget against actual** | Your budget next to what really happened, month by month, with the difference |
| **Financial ratios** | Simple health checks, compared with the previous period (below) |

### Financial ratios in plain words

| Question | Ratios |
| --- | --- |
| Can we pay our bills on time? | Current ratio, quick ratio, cash ratio |
| Are we making money? | Gross margin, net margin, return on assets, return on equity |
| How much do we depend on debt? | Debt ratio, debt to equity |
| How fast does money move? | Days customers take to pay, days we take to pay suppliers, days stock stays on the shelf |

If a ratio cannot be worked out (for example because there were no sales yet), it is left blank
rather than showing a misleading number.

## Dashboards

For a quick look without opening a report, the dashboards show cash available, what customers owe,
what you owe, payments waiting for approval, and how spending compares with the budget.
