# Miles Logbook

**Every shift, on the record.**

Miles Logbook replaces paper log books and Excel registers with digital records your team can
trust. Each record has a fixed structure, a reference number, an optional supervisor review and a
history nobody can edit. Logbook also runs the work around the log: tasks, inspections that are
due, problems to follow up and reminders.

It works in any browser and on an Android phone, with or without an internet connection.

## Your own forms, built in minutes

- **Form builder** with a live preview: build the log sheet your team fills in, without any
  technical help.
- **13 field types:** short and long text, whole and decimal numbers, date, time, date and time,
  single and multiple choice, yes/no, files and photos, a person, and a piece of equipment or a
  vehicle.
- **Rules on each field:** required or optional, help text, default values, limits, units
  (km, litres, hours, °C) and a **normal range** that flags unusual readings.
- **Questions that appear only when needed,** for example "Where is the leak?" only when "Leak
  found?" is Yes.
- **Ready-made templates** to start from: Daily Activity Log, Generator Operation Log, Vehicle
  Movement Log and IT Maintenance Log.
- **Versions:** changing a form never changes old records. Each entry keeps the version it was
  written on.
- **Logbooks** group the forms for one area of work, open to the whole team or restricted to the
  people you choose.

## Recording the work

- Fill in a log sheet on the web or the phone, save a draft and finish it later.
- Attach photos from the camera and documents (PDF, Word, Excel…).
- Every submitted entry gets a **reference number** in order, with no gaps, for example
  `GEN-2026-000042`.
- Unusual readings are **recorded as they are and flagged**, never refused or changed.
- Download any entry as a **PDF**.

## Review by a supervisor

- Choose for each log sheet whether its entries need review.
- Supervisors **mark entries reviewed** or **return them with a comment** for correction.
- Every submission is kept: a corrected entry still shows what was first sent.
- **Nobody can review their own entry.**

## Tasks and recurring work

- Give a task to a person with a **due time, priority and reminders**, linked to a logbook,
  entry, piece of equipment or problem.
- People **start and complete** tasks with notes, photos and evidence, on the web or the phone.
- Ask for **verification**: someone else confirms the work was done, or returns it.
- **Recurring tasks** every day, on chosen weekdays, or monthly, which can be paused, resumed or
  ended at any time.
- Overdue work is shown and notified.

## Inspections on a schedule

- Put any log sheet on a schedule, for example "the generator log every morning by 8:00".
- Logbook knows what was expected even when nobody wrote anything, and marks each one **on
  time, late, missed or excused**.
- See completion rates, and what was late or missed, at a glance.

## Problems followed to the end

- **Exceptions** open automatically for out-of-range readings and failed checks, or are
  reported by hand, with a severity from low to critical.
- Fix them with **corrective tasks**, then close them. **The person who fixed a problem cannot
  close it themselves.**

## Equipment, vehicles and places

- Keep your **resources** (generators, vehicles, fridges, rooms) and the **locations** they are
  in: sites, buildings, rooms.
- Pick them in entries and tasks, so every record says exactly what it is about.

## Location and trip routes (optional)

Switched off unless you turn it on for a log sheet.

- **Entry location:** the phone or browser takes the position of an entry when the person presses
  **Take location**, with its accuracy. Nothing is ever guessed.
- **Trip routes:** drivers and field staff **start, pause and end a trip** on the phone. The
  route is recorded even without a connection and sent when the phone is back online.
- Each route is shown on a **map** with its start, its end, the entries written along the way and
  any gaps in the recording, with distance, duration and the vehicle used.
- Filter routes by date, person, vehicle, logbook and log sheet, and **export them as GPX**.
- **Privacy by design:** tracking never starts by itself, the phone shows a notification the
  whole time a trip records, it asks only for location while the app is in use, and only
  authorised people can see other people's routes. Routes are deleted automatically after a set
  period.

## Notifications and reminders

- A **bell** in the app for what needs you: tasks given or returned, entries to review,
  inspections due or missed, problems assigned.
- **Email** for the important ones, and **phone notifications** on Android, each turned on or off
  by each person.
- Reminders before, at or after a due time, sent once, to whoever has the work at the time.

## Reports and exports

- Filter entries by logbook, log sheet, dates, person, status and review state, or search.
- See counts by status, day and person, and the **total, average, lowest and highest** of every
  number (fuel used, kilometres driven, hours run).
- **Export to CSV or PDF.**

## The phone app (Android)

- Write entries, do tasks and record trips **with no connection**. Everything is saved on the
  phone as it is typed and sent as soon as there is a signal.
- Nothing is counted as sent until the server confirms it, and nothing is ever sent twice.
- Photos, files, people and equipment pickers, the same rules as the web.
- Supervisors review entries on the go.
- Everything stored on the phone is **encrypted**, and each person's work is kept apart on a
  shared phone.

## Records you can trust

- **Nothing is overwritten.** Every version, review and action is kept.
- **A permanent audit trail:** who did what, and when, for logbooks, forms, entries, tasks,
  access and routes. It cannot be edited or deleted.
- **Separation of duties:** nobody reviews their own entry, verifies their own task or closes a
  problem they fixed.
- **Missed work is visible,** not just the work that was done.
- **Your data stays yours:** each organisation's records are completely separate, and files are
  shown only to people allowed to open the record.
