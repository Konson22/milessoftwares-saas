Roles that keep duties apart

RoleCan do
Organization AdministratorEverything, plus approvals, grants, settings and the audit log
Project ManagerEverything on projects; drafts budgets, requests spending; sees grants
Team MemberWorks tasks, logs own time, requests spending
ViewerView only
Finance ControllerApproves budgets and spending, records payments, manages grants
Finance OfficerRecords payments of approved spending
Funder contactTheir funder’s donor portal only
A member can hold more than one role, and the server checks every action again. Roles are assigned on the platform’s members page.
