What guides the product

Different hands

Whoever asks for money never approves or pays it. The product enforces that.

History stays

Numbered amendments, budgets that never change once approved, spending voided not deleted.

Figures that mean something

Committed kept apart from spent; currencies never added together.

Said plainly

What is not built is marked Planned and listed in one place.

Each organization’s records are kept to itself: a query made without an organization returns nothing. Every security-relevant change is in the audit log, which the app never edits.
