Everything in one menu

AreaWhat is in it
OverviewPortfolio, My work, search and notifications
ProjectsProjects, Programmes, Spaces and lists, People, Procurement
FundingGrants, Funders, partnerships with beneficiaries
FinanceApprovals, Commitments, Staff advances, Petty cash, Accounting
RecordsDocuments with every version kept
AdministrationMembers, Departments and teams, Roles, Settings, Audit log

Documents, Grants, Procurement and Accounting can each be turned off in Settings. Nothing is deleted.

The left-hand menu groups the product into six areas. Organizations switch off the parts they do not use, and switching back brings everything back.
