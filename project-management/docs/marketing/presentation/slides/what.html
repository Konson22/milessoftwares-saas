What it is

One workspace for the work, the money and the books — and a shared record of each grant with whoever funds it.

Plan

Projects, tasks, milestones, risks, team and time.

Control

Budgets, spending approvals, procurement and petty cash.

Report

Grants, donor reports and double-entry books that post as you pay.

An organization can run its own work with no donor at all. When one organization funds another, a grant is what connects their two workspaces.
