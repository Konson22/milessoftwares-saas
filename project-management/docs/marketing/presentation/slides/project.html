A project

Health is calculated, not guessed

Off track: past due, or over budget.

At risk: progress 20+ points behind time elapsed, overdue tasks, or spend 20+ points ahead of progress.

Each project has a code that numbers its tasks, a manager, dates, a budget and a status. The overview shows progress weighted by estimate, milestones, what is coming up, the top risks and the activity log. The 20-point slack can be changed by the organization.
