The life of a grant

1

Invite

The donor invites the organization. Nothing connects until it accepts.

2

Record

Award, amendments, allocations and payment schedule.

3

Choose

Turn on what to share: budget, spending, progress, tasks.

4

Deliver

Tasks move, purchases are approved, payments post to the books.

5

Report

Reports with due dates and documents, visible once submitted.

6

Accept

The donor accepts or asks for a revision. No report owed: it closes.

Steps one to three set the grant up; four to six are the delivery loop. A revision request sends the report back as owed.
