Three hands on every payment

1 · Request

A team member asks, against a budget category and grant, with quotes attached.

2 · Approve

Someone else commits it, only if it fits the budget and the grant. Bands of 1–3 approvers.

3 · Pay

Finance records date and reference; the journal posts in the same step.

The requester never approves or pays their own request; the system enforces it. Rejected, cancelled and voided spending stays on record. Finance, Approvals collects everything waiting for the person looking at it.
