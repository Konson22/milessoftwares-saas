Sharing with donors

The organization decides, one grant and one switch at a time

Can be shared, off by default

  • Approved budget by category
  • Paid spending and committed total
  • Progress, percent done, milestones
  • Task number, title, status, dates

Always stays inside

  • Suppliers and receipts
  • Who requested or approved
  • Staff and the cost of people
  • Risks, notes, task assignees
Every grant has its own switches and all are off until someone turns them on. Donors see only their own grants, even when an organization has several donors.
